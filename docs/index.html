About Brian

Brian Dominguez narrates audiobooks for independent authors. Since 2025 he has recorded fourteen titles for Audible, from espionage thrillers and science fiction to practical guides on money, business and relationships.

He voiced all four books of Tobey Alvarez's espionage series The Disavowed, so listeners hear one voice from the first book to the latest.

He also wrote Sam on the Way, letters from a father-to-be to his unborn son over the nine months before the birth. It first came out as a paperback in 2008, and Brian recorded the audiobook himself in 2026.
