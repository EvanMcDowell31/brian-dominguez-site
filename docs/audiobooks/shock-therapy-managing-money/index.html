About the book

When you absolutely need to figure it out.

Sick of financial advice that sounds like it was written by a robot? This isn’t that. Shock Therapy: Managing Money delivers blunt truths, sharp humor, and real strategies to help you stop surviving and start thriving.

Inside, you’ll:

  • Define what financial freedom means to you
  • Get brutally honest about where you stand
  • Learn how to earn more, spend smarter, and build lasting wealth
  • Break free from toxic money habits and comparison traps
  • Build a personalized gameplan you’ll actually follow

Packed with practical tools, eye-opening reality checks, satirical comics, and zero sugarcoating—this book is your no-BS guide to finally getting your financial life together.

Real talk. Real tools. Real freedom. You in?
