## About the book

Financial Freedom After Divorce: The 90-Day Checklist to Rebuild Your Finances and Regain Control

Divorce reshapes every part of your life, especially your finances. Managing bills, accounts, and budgets alone can feel overwhelming, and fear of missed payments and late fees adds stress.

Financial Freedom After Divorce is a 90-day guide to eliminate chaos and build a solid financial foundation. Unlike generic budgeting apps, it offers a clear, structured plan to get organized, avoid costly mistakes, and take control of your money.

Inside, you’ll discover:

- A 90-day checklist breaking essential tasks into manageable steps
- Strategies to avoid missed payments and protect your credit
- A simple framework for bill management and account tracking
- Actionable tools and real-life scenarios for every stage of rebuilding
- Cheatsheets including a payment tracker, budget planner, and quick-reference guide

Practical and backed by research, this guide helps you create lasting habits, prevent setbacks, and gain independence and confidence.

Why this book works:

- Designed for newly divorced professionals
- Time-bound 90-day structure ensures progress without overwhelm
- Focuses on practical action, not abstract advice
- Helps you make smart decisions today that protect your future

Each step fits into a busy lifestyle, proving you don’t need endless time or energy to get your finances back on track—just the right system.

By the end of 90 days, you’ll have a reliable plan, peace of mind, and confidence in your financial future.

Step into your new life with confidence—Financial Freedom After Divorce shows you the way.
