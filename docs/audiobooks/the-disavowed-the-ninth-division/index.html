About the book

Eight operatives.

One mission no one will claim.

And a truth powerful enough to bring governments to their knees.

When a nameless file surfaces across global intelligence networks—a dossier marked only with a red 9—eight agents from eight nations are quietly assembled under a false flag operation. Their orders: investigate the leak, neutralize whoever caused it, and erase all traces of involvement. Officially, The Ninth Division doesn’t exist.

But once the team discovers that the Division isn’t just a black-budget task force—it’s a cover for a vast, state-sanctioned network moving weapons and people under the guise of aid—the hunters become the hunted. Cut off from command, burned by their own governments, and outnumbered on every continent, they must decide whether to stay silent… or set the truth free.

As the mission unravels into betrayal, survival, and revelation, only three will make it out alive.

And the files they unleash will ignite a global reckoning no one can control.

Perfect for listeners who love:

  • Global conspiracy thrillers grounded in realism
  • Complex characters caught between duty and survival
  • The raw tension of Jason Bourne, Jack Ryan, and Body of Lies
  • Gritty, atmospheric prose and moral consequences

In a world built on deniability, truth is the most dangerous weapon of all.

The Ninth Division is the explosive first entry in Tobey Alvarez’s hard-edged espionage saga—where the lines between hero and traitor blur, and every mission leaves blood on the paper trail.
