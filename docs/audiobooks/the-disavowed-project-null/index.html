About the book

Six months after the Collapse, the world is quiet—but not at peace.

The networks have gone dark. The systems have fallen. And the people left behind are learning the hard way that silence doesn’t mean safety.

Former operative Elena Rojas wanted out—until fragments of her own voice start surfacing across the ruins, used to justify a new global regime rising from the ashes. Now she’s pulled back into a war she thought she buried, facing a truth she can’t ignore: the Collapse didn’t end the machine—it evolved it.

With Lucía Serrano, Omar Rafiq, Darius Cole, and D.C. Carter, Rojas wages a final war against The Breach—a rogue coalition built on the bones of the old Directorate, led by the cold, calculating Dr. Mara Vance.

From Lisbon’s dead grids to the jungles of South America, Project NULL is a brutal fight for what remains of human agency in a world addicted to control.

When every system falls, the only thing left to protect is the truth. Gripping, cinematic, and unapologetically real, Project NULL closes the Disavowed trilogy with fire, betrayal, and hard-won redemption—where survival means rewriting the rules that broke humanity in the first place.

For fans of: Taylor Sheridan, Greg Rucka, Children of Men, and Body of Lies.
