## About the book

After the fall of the Tribunal, the Ninth Division is scattered and disavowed.

Lucía Serrano leads what’s left of her team—D.C. Carter, Omar Rafiq, and Anya Kovács—through the shadows of a collapsing intelligence world. When a series of covert “stabilization leaks” points to a new black-budget program called Project Vanguard, they uncover the man behind it: Admiral Victor Kaelen, a war hero turned architect of psychological warfare.

Vanguard isn’t propaganda.

It’s control through fatigue—a global system that weaponizes misinformation until truth itself burns out.

Drawn back into the fire are former agents, fugitives, and ghosts from the Division’s past—and among them, a hunted Spanish analyst named Elena Rojas, whose leaked draft of the Vanguard Directive made her the world’s most wanted woman. Joined by cynical ex-Interpol operative Noah Keene, Lucía’s fractured team races across Europe to stop Kaelen’s silent war before it erases the very concept of accountability.

From the ruins of Geneva to the rooftops of a dying digital world, Project Vanguard dives into the war for perception itself—where data becomes doctrine, and heroes are rewritten in real time.

They can’t save the world.

They can only make lying expensive again.

A gritty, high-intensity geopolitical thriller blending espionage realism with psychological warfare and modern disinformation.

Fans of Jason Bourne, Body of Lies, and Deus Ex: Human Revolution will find the same fusion of intelligence intrigue and moral collapse.
