About the book

The Ninth Division was supposed to be finished.

Its agents disbanded. Its secrets buried. Its war over.

But when a leak in Zurich exposes the resurrection of Continuity—the covert global network Marcus Vale built to control truth itself—Lucía Serrano is pulled back into a fight she thought she’d escaped.

Reuniting with survivors D.C. Carter, Anya Kovac, and Emil Nowak, Lucía uncovers the Zurich Ledger, a document that ties governments, corporations, and humanitarian fronts to the same machine of manipulation she once destroyed. From rain-soaked alleys to marble boardrooms, the team hunts the architects of Continuity before they can rewrite history—and their lives—with a single directive.

What begins as exposure becomes survival. What was buried rises again.

And this time, the truth is the weapon.

Power doesn’t die—it just changes hands.
